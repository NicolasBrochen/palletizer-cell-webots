3D animation: Robotic palletizing cell

Robotic palletizing cell (Webots R2025a): one full cycle, from case infeed to pallet storage in the warehouse.

ABB IRB 4600-40 + 12-cup vacuum gripper, lot-forming buffer (2 cases), 4-layer Euro pallet, turntable stretch wrapper, exit through a light curtain with muting, forklift.
Left click: orbit · right click: pan · mouse wheel: zoom · bottom bar: play / scrub the timeline.

Source code: github.com/NicolasBrochen/palletizer-cell-webots
